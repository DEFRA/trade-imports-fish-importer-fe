# Fish import notifications

Shared business vocabulary for the fish importer notification journey.

## Language

**Transport mode**:
The means by which an imported fish consignment is transported: vessel, air freight or road vehicle.
_Avoid_: Transport method

**Draft notification**:
An import notification that has been saved but has not yet been submitted.
_Avoid_: Journey token

**Arrival**:
When and where the consignment is expected to arrive in Great Britain: its port of entry and expected arrival.

**Port of entry**:
The port or airport in Great Britain where the consignment arrives.
_Avoid_: Destination port, entry point

**Expected arrival**:
The date and time the consignment is expected to arrive at the port of entry, as given in UK local time.
_Avoid_: ETA, arrival time (when meaning the date as well)
